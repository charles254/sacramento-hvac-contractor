Frequently asked questions

How often should a gas furnace be inspected in Sacramento?

Gas furnaces should receive a comprehensive professional safety and combustion inspection annually in the autumn before initial winter use to confirm heat exchanger integrity and eliminate carbon monoxide hazards.

What are the warning signs of a cracked furnace heat exchanger?

Warning signs include soot accumulation around the burner compartment, a strong formaldehyde odor, a yellow or flickering burner flame instead of a crisp blue cone, and unprovoked carbon monoxide alarm activations.
